# Reusable principles — evidence required

1. Preserve 350 free lessons, 1,758 quizzes, 600 reading items and 96 professional lessons; counts alone do not prove educational quality or semantic fidelity.
2. Distinguish missing, held and approved human audio. Held audio must not play or be bypassed by synthetic fallback; missing audio may use clearly labeled synthetic speech only after explicit opt-in where policy allows.
3. Preserve immediate matching and booking state contracts across teams 13/14; test retries, cancellation, reconnect and reversals, not just happy paths.
4. Differentiate CI success, independent verification, integration success and actual Render staging99 deployment SHA/browser verification.
5. An environment failure and a product failure require different fixes. Keep strict audits and record counterexamples.

These are engineering rules, not claims that all acceptance tests have passed.
